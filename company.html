Company vision

Never let the limits of tools become the limits of creativity.
We don't constrain ideas to what tools can do; we create the means to make them real.

We deeply understand existing technologies, combine them, and extend them.
When necessary, we build entirely new systems from the ground up.

We don't start from “what is possible.”
We start from “what we want to make,” then expand what is possible.

Toward a world where creators' imaginations never become smaller
because of technology or environment.
